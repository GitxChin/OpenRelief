# Safety, Privacy, and Ethics

OpenRelief operates in a high-trust, high-risk humanitarian context. Safety, privacy, and ethics are product requirements, not optional features.

## Principles

- Minimize data collection.
- Use fictional examples in public repositories.
- Keep sensitive data restricted.
- Require human review for high-risk outputs.
- Preserve evidence and uncertainty.
- Avoid amplifying rumors.
- Protect vulnerable groups from exposure or discrimination.
- Do not publish personally identifiable help requests.

## Sensitive Data

Sensitive data may include:

- names;
- phone numbers;
- addresses;
- precise coordinates;
- medical conditions;
- disability status;
- photos of affected people;
- children or elderly people information;
- private chat logs;
- partner organization operations;
- rescue routes or restricted locations.

## AI Safety Rules

AI must not:

- claim a fact without a source;
- invent a location, contact, or organization;
- provide medical diagnosis or treatment;
- provide dangerous rescue instructions;
- expose private contact details in public outputs;
- downgrade urgent cases without human review;
- make final dispatch or triage decisions alone.

## Publication Rules

Public-facing maps, dashboards, and briefs should:

- remove direct identifiers;
- aggregate where possible;
- label verification status;
- show uncertainty;
- avoid exposing exact household locations;
- avoid publishing unresolved sensitive cases.

## Data Retention

Future implementation should define:

- retention windows;
- deletion processes;
- consent withdrawal;
- access logs;
- anonymization and aggregation procedures;
- backup and incident response policies.

## Fairness and Coverage

OpenRelief must account for people who may be underrepresented in digital channels:

- elderly people;
- children;
- people with disabilities;
- rural communities;
- migrants;
- people without stable internet;
- people who do not use public social media.

AI triage should be evaluated for missed urgent cases across these groups.
