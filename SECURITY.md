# Security Policy

OpenRelief handles humanitarian workflows that may involve sensitive personal, location, health, and operational information. Security reports are treated as high priority.

## Do Not Publicly Disclose

Do not open public issues containing:

- private personal information;
- phone numbers, addresses, ID numbers, or precise locations;
- medical or psychological details;
- access tokens, API keys, or credentials;
- internal NGO operating details;
- exploitable security vulnerabilities;
- unverified claims about real people needing rescue.

## Report Privately

Until a dedicated security contact is published, contact the project maintainers privately through the repository owner or trusted project channel.

Include:

- affected area or file;
- risk summary;
- reproduction steps if safe;
- potential impact;
- whether any sensitive data may have been exposed.

## AI Safety Reports

Please report AI behavior that may:

- leak personal information;
- invent facts or sources;
- produce unsafe medical, rescue, legal, or security advice;
- misclassify urgent life-safety cases;
- discriminate against vulnerable groups;
- encourage bypassing human review;
- expose operationally sensitive locations or partner information.

## Handling Policy

Maintainers should:

- acknowledge reports as soon as practical;
- avoid requesting unnecessary sensitive data;
- restrict access to reported details;
- document remediation decisions;
- publish a sanitized advisory when appropriate.
