# Acknowledgements

OpenRelief stands on the work of humanitarian practitioners, digital volunteers, disaster information service organizations, crisis informatics researchers, and open-source communities.

## Practice Foundations

We acknowledge the experience and methodology inherited from:

- NCP Relief, including online help-seeking workflows, volunteer coordination, medical and community support experience, and public assistance channels.
- Zhuoming Info Aid, including disaster information service practice, situation assessment, mapping, needs analysis, volunteer collaboration, and HEINA-style disaster information structuring.
- The existing emergency help mini-program practice, including public help requests, resource offers, field information reporting, disaster briefs, affected-area views, rescue organization information, and social force statistics.

## Research Foundations

OpenRelief is informed by research in AI for disaster response, crisis computing, social media situation awareness, emergency message classification, rescue scheduling, crowdsourcing, and automated disaster reporting.

We acknowledge the following papers and lines of work that shaped this repository's architecture and AI workflow design:

- **AIDR: Artificial Intelligence for Disaster Response**.
- **CrisisSense-LLM: Instruction Fine-Tuned Large Language Model for Multi-label Social Media Text Classification in Disaster Informatics**.
- **Using Social Media to Enhance Emergency Situation Awareness**.
- **A Deep Learning Approach for Tweet Classification and Rescue Scheduling for Effective Disaster Management**.
- **Socially Enhanced Situation Awareness from Microblogs Using Artificial Intelligence: A Survey**.
- **Harnessing Prompt-Based Large Language Models for Disaster Monitoring and Automated Reporting from Social Media Feedback**.
- **Intelligent Disaster Response via Social Media Analysis: A Survey**.
- **Using Artificial Intelligence to Identify Emergency Messages on Social Media During a Natural Disaster: A Deep Learning Approach**.

These works inform OpenRelief's emphasis on:

- human-in-the-loop classification and review;
- multi-label disaster information structuring;
- relevance and urgency triage;
- recall-first evaluation for life-safety signals;
- topic, trend, and location-aware situation awareness;
- source-grounded report drafting;
- privacy, safety, provenance, and auditability;
- digital volunteer collaboration.
