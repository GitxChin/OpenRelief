# AI Output Policy

AI output in OpenRelief is advisory. It must be reviewed according to risk level before being used for operations or publication.

## Allowed AI Uses

- summarize incoming records;
- structure help requests;
- classify needs;
- suggest urgency;
- extract location mentions;
- identify missing information;
- suggest duplicate records;
- suggest resource matches;
- draft internal notes;
- draft source-grounded situation briefs for review.

## Disallowed AI Uses

AI must not autonomously:

- dispatch rescue teams;
- provide medical diagnosis or treatment;
- provide legal advice;
- publish personal information;
- release public disaster reports;
- decide that a help request is fake;
- deny or close a high-risk case;
- contact affected people without human protocol;
- override partner or local command decisions.

## Output Requirements

AI outputs should include:

- structured result;
- confidence;
- uncertainty;
- source evidence;
- missing information;
- review requirement;
- safety notes.

## Public Output

Any public output must be reviewed for privacy, verification, and safety. Public summaries should be aggregated where possible.
