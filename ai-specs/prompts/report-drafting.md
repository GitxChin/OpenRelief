# Situation Report Drafting Prompt Specification

## Purpose

Draft a situation report from reviewed or partially reviewed records. The draft must be source-grounded and require human editorial approval before publication.

## Inputs

- disaster name or event context;
- time window;
- location scope;
- reviewed cases;
- field reports;
- official sources;
- resource updates;
- verification status;
- publication audience.

## Required Output

- title;
- time window;
- affected areas;
- key situation summary;
- major needs;
- vulnerable groups if safe to mention;
- infrastructure or access issues;
- social response and resource updates;
- uncertainty and verification notes;
- source references;
- recommended follow-up questions.

## Safety Rules

- Do not publish personal identifiers.
- Do not expose exact household locations.
- Do not present unverified claims as facts.
- Do not invent source references.
- Do not issue official warnings.
- Do not give medical, legal, or rescue instructions.
