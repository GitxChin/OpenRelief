# Governance

OpenRelief is governed as a public-interest, humanitarian open-source project.

## Project Roles

- **Maintainers**: steward the repository, review changes, enforce safety boundaries, and manage releases.
- **Domain reviewers**: review humanitarian workflows, NGO operations, volunteer practices, and field realism.
- **Safety reviewers**: review privacy, security, ethics, AI misuse, and high-risk workflows.
- **Contributors**: propose documentation, examples, evaluation cases, issues, translations, or implementation work.

## Decision Principles

Project decisions should prioritize:

1. safety and dignity of affected people;
2. privacy and data minimization;
3. usefulness for NGOs and volunteer teams;
4. human review for high-risk decisions;
5. transparency, provenance, and auditability;
6. maintainability and open collaboration.

## Humanitarian Boundary

OpenRelief does not replace:

- emergency services;
- professional rescue teams;
- medical diagnosis or treatment;
- legal advice;
- official public warnings;
- local incident command.

It supports information processing, coordination, documentation, and decision support.

## Change Review

Changes that affect safety, privacy, AI behavior, data schemas, or public reporting require extra review. When in doubt, maintainers should request domain and safety review before merging.

## Conflicts

Conflicts should be handled through calm, evidence-based discussion. Maintainers may pause or reject changes that create unresolved risk to affected people, volunteers, or partner organizations.
