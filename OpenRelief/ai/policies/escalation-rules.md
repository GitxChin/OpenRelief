# Escalation Rules

Escalation rules define when a case should be routed to a human coordinator, professional supporter, partner organization, or safety reviewer.

## Escalate to NGO Coordinator

- urgent or critical help request;
- unclear ownership;
- multiple organizations may be involved;
- high public visibility;
- conflicting reports;
- resource gap cannot be matched automatically.

## Escalate to Medical or Professional Support

- injury, illness, pregnancy, chronic disease, oxygen, medication, mental health crisis, or care needs;
- request for professional advice;
- risk of unsafe non-professional intervention.

## Escalate to Safety Reviewer

- personal data exposure;
- child or vulnerable-person information;
- public map or brief includes sensitive details;
- AI output suggests unsafe action;
- possible abuse, fraud, coercion, or exploitation.

## Escalate to Technical Maintainer

- system error affects intake, review, notification, or audit;
- possible data leak;
- prompt or model behavior creates systematic safety risk;
- evaluation failure in high-risk cases.

## Do Not Escalate Publicly

Sensitive cases should not be escalated through public GitHub issues or public chat channels.
