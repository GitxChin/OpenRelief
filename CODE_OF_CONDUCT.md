# Code of Conduct

OpenRelief is a humanitarian open-source project. Contributors are expected to act with care, respect, humility, and responsibility.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful toward affected communities, volunteers, NGOs, researchers, and contributors.
- Assume good intent while still reviewing claims carefully.
- Make space for field experience, technical expertise, and lived experience.
- Prefer clear, constructive feedback.
- Protect the dignity and privacy of people affected by disasters.
- Avoid sensationalizing suffering or using real cases without consent.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private or sensitive information without consent.
- Uploading real victim data, medical data, or contact details.
- Using the project to spread rumors, misinformation, or political attacks.
- Encouraging unsafe rescue, medical, legal, or security actions.
- Bypassing review for high-risk AI outputs.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers through the repository's private security or maintainer contact channel. Public issue threads should not be used for sensitive personal reports.

Maintainers may remove content, close issues, restrict participation, or take other action needed to protect the community and affected people.
